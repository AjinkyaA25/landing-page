About Your Coach

Ajinkya Argonda runs close to a 65% win rate in Dune: Imperium – Uprising (around 45% across the base game and other expansions), and placed 4th in one of the biggest events in the community, the MrBeast Dune: Imperium Invitational. He also runs a YouTube channel where he breaks down gameplay and strategy for players getting into the game.

A lot of coaching out there is aimed at players who are already strong and chasing tournament wins. That's not who he coaches. He works with beginner-to-intermediate players — people who know the rules but keep losing to the same deck-building mistakes, misread intrigue timing, or just don't yet see the board the way stronger players do. If that's you, he can close that gap a lot faster than trial and error will.
