Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to be an experienced player?

No. The coaching is primarily aimed at beginner-to-intermediate players who understand the rules and want to improve their strategic decision-making.

Is Uprising the main focus?

Yes. Uprising is the primary focus, and coaching can also cover Bloodlines, Conspiracy, and other relevant expansions or formats.

Can we focus on a specific strategy problem?

Absolutely. If you struggle with deck building, intrigue timing, combat, faction influence, endgame planning, or another specific concept, we can make the session about that problem.

Can you review a game I've already played?

Yes. The VOD Review is specifically designed for this. Send a recording and I'll identify the decisions and patterns most worth learning from.

Can you help with my homebrew version of Dune: Imperium?

Yes. We can discuss custom cards, balance, faction identity, power level, interactions, and whether a homebrew mechanic creates meaningful decisions or an overly dominant strategy.

Do I need Discord?

Yes for live and strategy sessions, since those sessions are conducted over Discord.

How quickly will I receive a VOD Review?

Please allow at least 3 days for the completed review.
